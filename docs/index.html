What it does

⚙️ Five real output formats

PNG for lossless quality and transparency, JPG for smaller photo files, WebP and AVIF for modern compression, PDF for a print-ready document — or just save the original with no conversion at all.

⚡ One click, every time

Set your favorite format and folder once. After that, the right-click menu shows a single "Save as [format]" shortcut that saves instantly — no dialog, no digging through a submenu.

📐 The real image, not the thumbnail

Modern pages load an image several different ways — srcset, <picture>, a small preview linked to a larger original. Save Image As checks all of them and saves the highest-resolution version actually available.

🔒 100% local, private by design

Every conversion happens directly on your device. Images are never uploaded, never sent to a server, and never seen by us. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Read the full details on the privacy policy page.

Three steps to get started

  1. Pin it — click the puzzle-piece icon in your Chrome toolbar and pin Save Image As.
  2. Set your defaults — open the popup and choose your save mode, format and folder.
  3. Right-click any image — pick a format, or use the one-click shortcut.
